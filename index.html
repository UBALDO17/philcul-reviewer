Key Concepts (Handout 04)
Attraction
The main reason people visit. A place of interest due to natural beauty, historical, or cultural significance. Values: historical importance, cultural value, political significance, nature, built beauty, leisure & amusement.
Destination (UNWTO)
"The place visited that is central to the decision to take the trip." A destination often has more than one attraction and earns money from tourism.
Key Difference
A destination is an area that earns money from tourism; an attraction is a specific place that draws tourists. Example: Manila (destination) → Rizal Park & Intramuros (attractions).

Luzon
NCRNational Capital Region▾
Key Facts
Also known as Metropolitan Manila. Only region WITHOUT a province. Composed of 16 cities + 1 municipality (Pateros). PH's political, economic & educational center. Main airport: NAIA (between Parañaque & Pasay).

ManilaCapital of PHEver Distinguished Loyal City
Intramuros Heritage
Oldest & most historic place in Manila. Name means "within the walls." Encircled by high stone walls with castle-like earthworks. Contains Fort Santiago & San Agustin Church.
Fort Santiago
One of the oldest fortifications in Manila. Built by Spaniards in 1571. Now a memorial to WWII victims — it also served as a prison where Rizal was confined before his execution.
San Agustin Church UNESCO
Oldest church in the Philippines. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Part of the Baroque Churches of the Philippines group.
Rizal Park (Luneta)
Premiere people's park. World-class sculptures, historical markers, beautiful gardens. Flagship of freedom and courage — venue for political rallies, oath-takings, and major national events.
National Museum of the Philippines
Government agency with research & conservation programs. Houses: National Museum of Fine Arts, National Museum of Anthropology, National Museum of Natural History, & National Planetarium.
Manila Ocean Park 1st Marine Theme Park in PH
Country's first world-class marine theme park. Integrated urban resort with aquatic life attractions and aqua-themed hotel. Features the Oceanarium (walkthrough of marine creatures indigenous to PH & SE Asia), Sea Lion Show, Star Bird Show.
Quezon City—City of Stars and New Horizons
Quezon Memorial Circle
27-hectare national park dedicated to President Manuel L. Quezon. A monument at the center has a museum with Quezon's memorabilia, and his remains are enshrined inside.
Art in Island Museum
3D art museum founded by Yun Jae Kyoung and 18 Korean master painters. Art gives an illusion of depth when viewed from a specific angle — designed as backdrops for interactive photos.

Key Facts

ProvinceCapitalNicknameKey Attractions & Details
AbraBangued—
Kapakan Falls
Scenic waterfall attraction in Abra province.
ApayaoKabugao—
Lussok Crystal Cave
Cave known for its crystal formations, one of Apayao's natural wonders.
BenguetLa TrinidadSalad Bowl of the Philippines
Baguio Cathedral
A familiar Baguio landmark with a distinct pink facade, rose windows, and twin square belfry. Completed in 1936. One of the most photographed structures in Baguio.
Burnham Park
The city's premier park and oldest park in Baguio. Gardens and a man-made lake at the city center. Activities include biking, skating, boarding, and boating.
Wright Park
Popular for horseback riding and souvenir shopping. A long stone stairway leads to the "Pool of Pines" — a 100-meter-long pool lined with Baguio pine trees. Located opposite the main gate of The Mansion (official summer residence of the President of the Philippines; has a mini museum of former presidents' memorabilia).
Colors of Stobosa
Hillside home artwork in La Trinidad, Benguet. Magic colors transformed stone-hill houses into an artwork symbolizing "re-blooming" and "re-greening" of the environment. A global transformation icon.
Mines View Park & Mt. Pulag
Mines View Park — overlooks the gold, silver, and copper mines of Benguet. Mt. Pulag — 2nd highest mountain in PH; famous for its sea of clouds above the clouds phenomenon.
IfugaoLagawe—
Banaue Rice Terraces UNESCO
Dubbed the "Eighth Wonder of the World." UNESCO World Heritage Site. Cascading rice terraces carved into mountainsides 2,000 years ago by Ifugao ancestors using only primitive tools. Spread over the Cordillera mountain range.
Philippine War Memorial Shrine (Kiangan)
Gigantic pyramid-type concrete structure resembling an oversized Ifugao native house in Linda, Kiangan. Commemorates the end of WWII. Depicts Filipino opposition to the Death March and the role of Ifugao and American troops in defeating General Yamashita.
KalingaTabuk—
Apo Whang-Od (Buscalan Village) Living Treasure
Home of the last and oldest mambababatok (traditional Kalinga tattoo artist). For over 70 years, Apo Whang-Od has been tattooing Kalinga warriors and women during rites of passage (childbirth, adulthood, marriage, eldership, ancestorship). Tools: soot + water pigment, calamansi thorn on bamboo stick, tapper stick, dried bamboo grass as template.
Mountain ProvinceBontoc—
Sugong Hanging Coffins / Echo Valley (Sagada)
Locals place their deceased in coffins and transport them to a cave for burial — a unique burial tradition where coffins are hung on cliff sides. Located in Echo Valley, Sagada.
Sumaguing Cave
One of the Philippines' most famous spelunking sites. Massive cave with impressive rock formations in Sagada, Mountain Province.
Bontoc Museum
Features artifacts for domestic and traditional purposes. Beside the museum is a model of a traditional Bontoc village highlighting Bontoc material culture.

ProvinceCapitalNicknameKey Attractions & Details
Ilocos NorteLaoag—
Paoay Church (Saint Augustine Church) UNESCO
UNESCO World Heritage Site. One of the Baroque Churches of the Philippines. Known for its massive earthquake-proof buttresses and Baroque-Romanesque architecture.
Malacañang of the North
National park and museum in Paoay. Originally the official residence of President Ferdinand Marcos Sr. when visiting his home province of Ilocos Norte.
La Paz Sand Dunes
Sandy coastal desert near Laoag City. Activities: sand surfing and ATV riding through the dunes. One of the largest sand dunes in Southeast Asia.
Bangui Windmills
Row of giant wind turbines along the coastline of Bangui, Ilocos Norte — the first wind farm in Southeast Asia. A scenic landmark and eco-tourism site.
Ilocos SurViganHeritage City of the Philippines
Vigan City UNESCO
Most well-preserved Spanish colonial town in the Philippines. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Built in the 16th century. Boasts colonial-era houses, horse-drawn carriages (kalesa), and Baroque buildings. Even big brands (7-Eleven, McDonald's) adopt traditional colonial building designs to blend in.
Calle Crisologo
Famous cobblestone street lined with Spanish-era houses. Tourists walk along horse-drawn carriages and rustic mansions showcasing Vigan's rich culture and history.
Bantay Bell Tower
Iconic symbol of Vigan. Built in 1591 as a watchtower to guard against pirates and invaders. Offers a great panoramic view of surrounding towns.
Padre Burgos House (NM Ilocos Regional Museum)
One of three historical buildings of the NM Ilocos Regional Museum Complex. Ancestral house constructed in 1788. Declared historical due to its association with Fr. Jose Burgos, whose death (along with 2 other priests — GOMBURZA) sparked the anti-Spanish colonial resistance movement.
La UnionSan FernandoSurfing Capital of the North
San Juan Beaches (Surfing)
La Union is famous for its surf spots, particularly in San Juan town, drawing surf enthusiasts from all over the country.
Baluarte Watch Tower (Luna)
Historic watchtower in Luna, La Union — a remnant of the Spanish colonial defense system.
PangasinanLingayen—
Hundred Islands National Park (Alaminos) National Park
A cluster of 124 islands and islets in Lingayen Gulf. Named for the approximate number of islands visible at low tide. Excellent snorkeling, beach hopping, and kayaking. Governor's Island, Children's Island, and Quezon Island are the most visited.
📍 Dagupan City = Bangus (Milkfish) Capital · Lingayen = Most Romantic Place in PH

BatanesBascoLand of True Insulars
Ruins of Songsong
Ghost barangay in Uyugan, Batan island — a cluster of abandoned old houses with roofless shells after a devastating tidal wave hit the island in the 1950s.
Honesty Coffee Shop
Unmanned refreshment store famous worldwide for showcasing Ivatan honesty. Buyers get goods and leave payment in a wooden box — completely on the honor system with no attendant present.
Sabtang Island
One of the most beautiful islands in Batanes. Features intermittent sand beaches, steep mountains, and deep canyons. Home to traditional Ivatan stone houses.
CagayanTuguegarao—
Callao Cave
Seven-chambered cave in Peñablanca. Has massive limestone formations, 7 skylights, and a chapel inside. Also noted as the home to the oldest human remains found in the Philippines (the Callao Man, dated at ~67,000 years old).
Cagayan River (Rio Grande de Cagayan)
Longest and widest river in the Philippines — PH's mightiest watercourse, flowing from Nueva Vizcaya to the Cagayan valley.
Palaui Island
7,000-hectare protected island and serene ecotourism sanctuary. Virgin ecosystems, geological formations, rich terrestrial and aquatic wildlife.

Aguinaldo Shrine & Giant Butaka
Landmark in Isabela connected to Philippine revolutionary history.

Lower Magat Eco-Tourism Park
Eco-tourism park near the Magat Dam, one of the largest dams in the Philippines, offering nature tours and outdoor activities.

Memorializes the bravery of Filipino and American forces against the Japanese. Approximately 72 hectares from foot to cross. Features a Colonnade (row of tall columns) and a huge Memorial Cross visible from miles away.

Region IV-ACALABARZON▾
Key Facts
"Industrial Powerhouse of the Philippines". SW Luzon, south & east of Metro Manila. Batangas, Cavite & Laguna = first to revolt vs. Spain.
Province → Capital → Nickname → Key Attractions with Details
ProvinceCapitalNicknameKey Attractions & Details
BatangasBatangas City—
Taal Volcano
Smallest active volcano in the Philippines. Located on an island within Taal Lake, which is itself on a larger island (Luzon) — making it a lake within a volcano on an island. Visitors ride a boat to the foot, then trek or ride a horse to the crater top.
Fortune Island
Famous for crumbling pillars called "Acropolis" (inspired by the Acropolis of Athens). Once a private luxury island resort — now abandoned due to lack of fresh water and typhoon exposure. A popular snorkeling and camping spot.
Anilao
Popular diving sites brimming with marine life. Known as the Nudibranch Capital of the Philippines — famous among underwater photographers for its colorful sea slugs.
CaviteImus—
People's Park in the Sky & Sky Ranch (Tagaytay)
People's Park in the Sky — the unfinished mansion of Imelda Marcos, now a tourist park atop a hill with a panoramic view of Taal Lake. Sky Ranch — amusement park in Tagaytay with a Ferris wheel offering views of Taal Volcano.
LagunaSanta Cruz—
Pagsanjan Falls
Skilled boatmen take visitors on "shooting the rapids" — maneuvering through the turbulent waters of Magdapio River to reach the falls. Tourists can also go under the falls on bamboo rafts. Featured in Francis Ford Coppola's film Apocalypse Now.
Enchanted Kingdom
First and only world-class theme park in the Philippines. Located in Santa Rosa. Unique rides and attractions. One of the most visited family destinations in the country.
Pansol Hot Springs
Hot spring resorts warmed by Mt. Makiling (a dormant volcano). Numerous resort complexes offering private pool rentals with naturally heated mineral water.
QuezonLucena—
Beaches & Islands
Quezon province has numerous beach and island destinations including Cagbalete Island and Bondoc Peninsula beaches.
RizalAntipoloCradle of Philippine Arts
Angono–Binangonan Petroglyphs Oldest Filipino Art
Discovered by National Artist Carlos "Botong" Francisco in 1965. The most ancient Filipino work of art — dates back to the Stone Age. Famous for 127 rock engravings of human and animal figures on a rockwall in Binangonan, Rizal.
Daranak Falls
Nestled at the foot of the Tanay mountain range. Natural vegetation, forest trees, flowering wild plants, cascading waterfalls, streams, and ditches. Popular picnic and swimming site.
Higantes Festival (Angono)
Secular celebration to honor patron Saint Clement. Parade of giant papier-mâché figures (higantes) held on November 22 and 23. Angono is also known as the Arts Capital of the Philippines.
Mt. Pamitinan (Rodriguez, Rizal)
Nestled in the Sierra Madre Mountain ranges. Spectacular summit view with limestone crags and marble boulders providing various difficulty levels for climbers.
Tanay Sea of Clouds
A popular viewpoint in Tanay, Rizal where you can witness a stunning sea of clouds phenomenon at sunrise.
